One app, a little more in your corner.

From cycle notes to daily sips, PeriBuds brings the things you want to keep track of into one calm place.

🌹

Know your cycle

Log your period and see cycle patterns, expected dates, fertile-window estimates and ovulation estimates in a clear calendar.

👟

Walk your way

Automatic step counting with a goal you choose. Progress moves in 8-step increments; there are no manual step edits.

💧

Keep water in view

Log a glass in a tap, set your own daily target and see your intake build through the day.

🥗

Your meals, your routine

Make a meal chart that fits your day, mark meals done and choose the reminders you want.

📊

Patterns you can share

Review cycle history and averages, then create a report you can save or share.

⚙️

Set it your way

Manage activity and notification access, choose reminders and find app credits in Settings.

🧳

Plan around travel

Add a trip to see if it overlaps with an expected period, with a handy packing checklist.

🌤️

Notice how you feel

Keep a simple daily record of symptoms and mood, then look back for patterns across cycles.

🔒

Private by design

No account, cloud sync or ads. Your cycle and wellbeing records stay in the app’s local storage.

sip ✦

Small steps count, too.

Choose any step goal that feels right. PeriBuds counts automatically after you allow activity access, then groups your progress into 8-step increments. You’ll also see an estimated calorie burn based on your recorded steps.

  • Your goal can be any positive whole number
  • Steps come from your phone’s activity sensor
  • Water logging and its target are yours to set

Good to know

A few plain answers before you install.

Does PeriBuds need an account or internet?

No account or cloud sync. The app’s core tracking is designed to work offline, and your records are stored locally on your phone.

Can I set any step goal?

Yes. Choose any positive whole-number goal. The step display advances in 8-step increments, and steps are counted automatically after activity permission is granted.

Does the app tell me how much water to drink?

No. You choose your own water target and record your intake. The tracker does not prescribe a daily amount.

Will this update an earlier PeriBuds install?

Yes. This APK is signed with the same certificate as the previous build, so it can be installed over it while keeping the app’s local data.

Is an iPhone version available?

This release is an Android APK. It can’t be installed on iPhone.
