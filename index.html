Good to know

Straight answers, no fine print.

Is PeriBuds really free?

Yes — free to download, free to use, no ads, no subscriptions, forever.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline, on your phone. Your data never leaves your device.

Is my information private?

Yes. There is no account and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your cycles and diet stay on your phone, and uninstalling the app erases them — so keep a backup of anything you need.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet — the iPhone build is made on a Mac and will be added here as an .ipa file for sideloading. Android is ready today.

Will reminders say "period" on my lock screen?

No. Reminders are worded discreetly — a gentle nudge, never the word "period" in the notification itself.
